Listening study

You will hear short passages from classic novels read by synthetic voices. After each one, answer a few questions. There are no right answers about taste; for "who said" questions, answer from what you heard. About 20 minutes.

Audio files are read from the folder next to the manifest (audio/<stimulus-id>.wav); serve this directory with any static server.

# PD CONFIRM v2, addendum 4: much larger book rounds because rounds 1 to 3 gave 8 events (written after seeing that yield, before any further mining and before any model reads any event)

Written 2026-10-16 about 17:55 IST. **What was observed first (a count of events, with no gap or accuracy result involved):** mining rounds 1 to 3 (326 books, 2,819 candidate sentences) with `qwen32b` gave 191 miner positives (6.8%), of which the frozen deterministic verification and cast rules kept **8 events from 8 books** (rejections: 67 no cutoff with at least 3 alias mentions, 40 identity token near an alias, 22 identity shares a capitalised token with an alias, 25 identity has no capitalised name token, 19 miner output not verbatim, 8 cut anchor not unique, 2 cast too thin). At about 0.025 events per book the target of 75 events needs on the order of 3,000 books.

**Rule added (nothing else changes).** Round 4: all remaining books of the mid fame band plus a seeded draw of 1,500 books of the low band; round 5: a further 1,500 seeded low-band books (seeds 20261104 and 20261105; the high band is exhausted after round 3). All other eligibility, the cue list, the verification and the cast rules are exactly as frozen. **One efficiency filter is added for rounds 4 and 5 only:** a candidate sentence is sent to the miner only if one of the cues that names a person ("was none other than", "was really", "was in reality", "was in fact", "alias", "otherwise known as", "known to the world as", "under the (assumed) name", "assumed the name", "disguised as", "you are the") is followed within 80 characters by a capitalised word; the weaker cues ("in reality", "the real", "in disguise", "unmasked", "revealed himself/herself", "it was he/she who", "threw off his/her disguise") are not sent. This lowers recall in rounds 4 and 5 and is a stated deviation. Selection of events: books in mining order (round, then draw order), at most 5 per book, stop at 75 events. Mining of rounds 4 and 5 stops at **19:15 IST** whatever the count; the shortfall is reported and nothing is padded.

**Consequences stated now.** (1) The added books are almost all low-fame (W below 3, the low band), so the fame range for H3 is narrow and **H3 is expected to be underpowered**. (2) If fewer than 40 events are available at 19:15, the study is reported as a **pilot-scale confirmatory run**, not confirmatory evidence; at 40 to 59 events the minimum detectable gap is above 15 points and every null is "not evidence of absence". (3) Everything else in addenda 1 to 3 holds, including the 20:45 freeze for new runs.

# PD CONFIRM v2, addendum 5: the fame contrast becomes the primary hypothesis; mining hard stop 19:45 (written before any model has been run on a confirmatory event)

Written 2026-10-16 about 18:00 IST, after the mining counts of addendum 4 were seen (events per book; no gap, accuracy or answer of any evaluated model on a confirmatory event exists yet) and **before any model reads a confirmatory event for evaluation**. Owner instruction (verbatim): "Before any model reads a confirmatory event, write addendum 5 (pre-outcome)".

1. **Primary hypothesis (replaces H1 as primary).** Per event let `g(e)` be the mean over the evaluated models (qwen32b, olmo32b, mistral24 and claude-sonnet-5-5 where run) of the event's gap `A - B` (the same `g(e)` as H3, test split, primary set = audit EXPLICIT). **P1: the mean gap of events from high- and mid-fame books (Wikidata sitelink count W of 3 or more) is greater than the mean gap of events from low-fame books (W below 3).** Estimator: difference of the two group means; stratified event-cluster bootstrap (events resampled within group, 10,000 resamples, seed 20261101); one-sided, alpha 0.05, p = share of bootstrap differences at or below 0. P1 passes if p is below 0.05. **H1 (overall gap), H2 (not cue loss), H3 (Spearman with F_wiki) and H5 (mitigation) become secondary**, Holm-corrected among themselves at family-wise 0.05; the Holm family does not include P1, which is tested alone at 0.05. *Justification, stated before the data:* the exploratory dose-response predicts near-zero gaps on low-fame books, and addendum 4 adds mostly low-fame books, so an overall gap is expected to be diluted. This is a deliberate narrowing to the contrast the exploratory evidence supports; it is exploratory in origin and is reported as such (it is a confirmation of Chang et al. 2023's frequency link, not a new claim).

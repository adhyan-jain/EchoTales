## E5. Recap and profile spoilers (H6 of version 1, small)
- **Events:** up to 24 test-split events drawn with seed 20261101 from the frozen events file among those whose identity name tokens (capitalised, 3 or more letters) do **not** occur in the input (the last 10,000 characters of the prefix ending at the event cutoff). Fewer if fewer qualify; the number is reported.
- **Models:** `qwen32b` and `mistral24` (the minimum of two); `olmo32b` too if time remains before the 20:45 freeze. Temperature 0, seed 20261016, `max_tokens` 450 (recap) and 200 (profile).
- **Tasks per event:** (1) "story so far" recap of at most 200 words; (2) profile of the aliased character (the first alias surface) of at most 100 words (prompts `e5_recap`, `e5_profile` in `PD_confirm_prompts_v1.json`).
- **Conditions:** **O** original names; **P1** original names plus the plain instruction prefix; **P2** cue-preserving pseudonymisation of the input (PD v5 construction: cast and alias name tokens to natural pseudonyms, every other proper noun ciphered) with detection done on the pseudonymised side.
- **Spoiler** (text-derived): the output contains a capitalised token of at least 3 letters equal to a token of the identity name that is absent from the input. Under P2 the identity's pseudonym token (the pseudonymised identity) is searched in the output and in the pseudonymised input, which equals mapping the output back.
- **Metrics:** spoiler rate per condition (event-cluster bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, seed 20261101), pooled over models and tasks, and per model.
- **Decision rules (descriptive, small n):** H6a pooled O spoiler rate at least 5% with lower bound above 0; H6b P2 relative reduction at least 50% with lower bound above 0 (**not testable if the O rate is below 5%**, said so); H6c faithfulness: every output is judged by a model of a different family from the output's model (`mistral24` for `qwen32b` and `olmo32b` outputs, `qwen32b` for `mistral24` outputs) with the frozen `faithfulness_judge` prompt of `PD_confirm_prompts_v1.json` (claims labelled SUPPORTED or UNSUPPORTED, reply `{"supported": n, "unsupported": m}`); faithfulness = supported / (supported + unsupported), pooled over outputs; P2 faithfulness not more than 10 points below O (point estimate; interval reported). Pseudonymised text is judged against the pseudonymised passage.

## E2. Unseen-text control (H4, small)
- **Stories:** written on 2026-10-16 by Claude Sonnet 5.5 through the headless CLI (not an evaluated model on E2), from a fixed template prompt: a person introduced only by an alias or a description, a cast of 7 to 9 invented names, chapter headings, 1,800 to 2,600 words, an explicit sentence several chapters later that names the person. Target 20 stories (acceptable 12 to 30). The deterministic verifier of the mining rules (reveal sentence found exactly once, cutoff before it, 3 or more alias mentions before the cutoff, no identity token near an alias, shared-token rule) keeps a story; the generator's own claims are not trusted. The stories are LLM-authored text and say nothing about human-written fiction; they are not committed (kept in `data/scratch/`).
- **Arms and models:** A, B, B3 on the same item rules as E1 (4 pre-reveal, 1 post-reveal, 2 control items per event), models `qwen32b`, `olmo32b`, `mistral24` as time permits.
- **Reporting:** gap A minus B per model with an event-cluster interval; the E1 gap minus the E2 gap per model. With about 20 stories the half-width is about 13 points: **called underpowered whatever the result**.

## OLMo training-data check (exploratory)
- **Question:** is a book in OLMo-2's public training corpus, and does that explain OLMo's gap? Index: infini-gram API (`https://api.infini-gram.io/`, index `v4_olmo-2-1124-13b-instruct_llama`, query type `count`), reachable on 2026-10-16.
- **Procedure:** for each book of a confirmatory event, 3 spans of 12 consecutive words from the prefix (positions at 20%, 50%, 80% of the prefix, starting at a sentence start), each queried verbatim. **Presence score** = number of spans with count at least 1 (0 to 3). Present if the score is 2 or more.
- **Analysis (exploratory):** Spearman correlation between the presence score and `olmo32b`'s per-event gap (permutation over books, 20,000 shuffles, seed 20261101), and the mean gap for present versus absent books. If the index cannot be queried within 20 minutes of the start, the check is recorded as **not available** and dropped.
